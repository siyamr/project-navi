========================================
CHAPTER 1: Kokiri Forest
========================================
You start your adventure inside your treehouse in the heart of the village.

--- Finding the Kokiri Sword ---
Head to the southwest corner of the village and crawl through the small tunnel.
Watch out for the rolling boulder patrolling the maze.

--- Buying the Deku Shield ---
Collect 40 Rupees from bushes and rooftops, then buy the shield at the Kokiri Shop.

========================================
CHAPTER 2: Inside the Deku Tree
========================================
--- Breaking the Central Web ---
Climb the vines to the upper ledge and jump straight onto the spider web in the center.

--- Boss: Queen Gohma ---
Stun her eye with the Slingshot when it turns red, then strike with your sword.

========================================
CHAPTER 3: Hyrule Castle
========================================
Courtyard Stealth
-----------------
Stay behind the hedges to avoid the patrolling guards until you reach Princess Zelda.
