========================================
CHAPTER 1: Kokiri Forest
========================================
You start your adventure in your treehouse at the heart of the village.

--- Finding the Kokiri Sword ---
Follow the southwest side of the village and crawl through the small tunnel.
Watch out for the rolling boulder inside the maze.

--- Buying the Deku Shield ---
Collect 40 rupees from bushes and platforms, then visit the shop.

========================================
CHAPTER 2: Deku Tree
========================================
--- Traversing the Floors ---
Climb the vines and jump from the ledge onto the central spider web.

--- Boss: Gohma ---
Aim at her eye with the slingshot as soon as it turns red, then strike with your sword.

========================================
CHAPTER 3: Hyrule Castle
========================================
Sneaking Through the Courtyard
------------------------------
Avoid the guards by staying behind the hedges until you reach Princess Zelda.
